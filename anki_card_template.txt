VietLearnTTS — card template with audio preview
================================================
In Anki:  Browse -> click any VietLearnTTS note -> "Cards..." button
Paste each block into the matching pane, then close. Applies to all 8,345 notes.

Both play buttons sit on the FRONT so you can A/B male vs female on the same
word without flipping. An empty sound field renders as nothing, so notes with
no female audio just show one button.

------------------------- FRONT TEMPLATE -------------------------
<div class="vn">{{Vietnamese_Text}}</div>

<div class="lbl">male</div>
{{Vietnamese_Male_Sound}}

<div class="lbl">female</div>
{{Vietnamese_Female_Sound}}

------------------------- BACK TEMPLATE --------------------------
{{FrontSide}}

<hr id=answer>

<div class="en">{{English_Text}}</div>
<div class="id">{{ID}}</div>

==================================================================

Anki turns any field containing [sound:...] into a play button automatically,
so no extra markup is needed — just referencing the field is enough.

To audit the 53 gi- words, paste this into the Browse search bar first:

note:VietLearnTTS (Vietnamese_Text:gi* OR Vietnamese_Text:gì OR Vietnamese_Text:gìn) -"Vietnamese_Text:* *"
